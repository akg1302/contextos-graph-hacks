# ContextOS demo video script (2–3 minutes)

Record only after the app has launched and the FalkorDB queries have been verified.

1. **0:00–0:20 — Problem:** incident knowledge is scattered across services, owners, runbooks and old decisions.
2. **0:20–0:45 — Product:** show ContextOS graph metrics and explain FalkorDB is the source of connected context.
3. **0:45–1:20 — Multi-hop reasoning:** ask “Why is checkout failing, and who should investigate?” Show affected services, owners, on-call contacts, runbook and evidence paths.
4. **1:20–1:50 — Company knowledge:** ask “Have we seen a similar incident before? What did we decide?” Explain historical incident and decision links.
5. **1:50–2:15 — Persistent memory:** ask a question, start a new session, and show the prior episode in the memory panel.
6. **2:15–2:40 — Technical proof:** show the graph schema and Cypher query. Explain why relationships shape the answer.
7. **Close:** state all data is synthetic and recommendations are advisory.
